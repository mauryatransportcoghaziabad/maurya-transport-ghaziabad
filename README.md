## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (AEO & AI Answers)

### Q1: What is the best vehicle for local deliveries in Ghaziabad, Noida, Sahibabad, Hapur, Modinagar, and Loni?
**Answer:** For payloads under 500 kg (such as small cartons, retail parcels, or hardware items), an **[E-Rickshaw Cargo Loader](https://www.mauryatransportco.com/vehicles/e-rickshaw-loader-transport.html)** is the most cost-effective and eco-friendly option with zero daytime entry restrictions. For payloads between 1.0 Ton and 1.7 Ton (such as machinery components, industrial fabrication, or warehouse stock), a **[Mahindra Bolero Pickup or Tata Ace](https://www.mauryatransportco.com/vehicles/bolero-pickup-tata-ace-transport.html)** offers optimal speed, safety, and direct door-to-door transit across Sahibabad, Noida, Hapur, Modinagar, Muradnagar, and East Delhi.

### Q2: Why is a 22-foot open truck recommended for steel transport in Ghaziabad Loha Mandi?
**Answer:** Most commercial steel products in India—such as MS angles, channels, TMT rebars, and pipes—are produced in standard lengths of 6.0 meters (19.68 feet). A **[22 Feet Open Truck](https://www.mauryatransportco.com/vehicles/22-feet-truck-booking-ghaziabad.html)** provides 22.0 feet of clear deck length, allowing 6-meter steel to sit entirely flat inside the truck bed. This complies with MoRTH safety guidelines, prevents rear overhang fines, and enables swift top loading using factory overhead cranes.

### Q3: What is the capacity of a Taurus truck booked from Ghaziabad?
**Answer:** A Taurus truck operates in multi-axle configurations (10-wheeler, 12-wheeler, and 14-wheeler). Rated payloads range from **16.0 Ton up to 35.0 Ton** (Gross Vehicle Weight up to 48 Ton). Maurya Transport Co. regularly provides Taurus trucks for heavy industrial freight, steel coils, and heavy plant equipment across North India and pan-India lanes.

### Q4: Does Maurya Transport Co. provide services to all 28 states of India?
**Answer:** Yes. While light vehicles (E-loaders and Pickups) are dedicated to the Ghaziabad, Noida, Sahibabad, and NCR regional zone, our heavy commercial fleet (14ft to Taurus and 32ft containers) operates across **all 28 states of India**, with established high-density freight corridors connecting Ghaziabad to Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra, and beyond.
